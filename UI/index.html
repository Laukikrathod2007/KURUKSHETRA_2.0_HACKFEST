Performant & Explainable Multi-Agent Defense Pipeline

01

Intent NLP Parsing

Reads payment memos and detects urgency, authority impersonation, extortion, and coercive social engineering patterns in real time.

02

Tiered Latency ML Scoring

114-feature LightGBM GBDT hot-path model delivering sub-15ms risk scoring on payee velocity and historical behavioral baselines.

03

Parallel Multi-Agent Consensus

Four specialized LangGraph agents (Intent, Transaction, Recipient, Policy Gatekeeper) evaluate context concurrently.

04

Cross-Bank Mule Graph Profiling

Continuously tracks recipient account age, rapid fan-out velocity spikes, and money mule network clusters across banks.

05

Cognitive Dwell Gate

Deploys calibrated psychological friction, pausing high-risk transfers before money leaves the victim's account.

06

Voice Guardian Emergency Call

Triggers an instant real-time AI voice conversation (Vapi AI) to speak directly with the user and break scammer phone coercion.

07

Tamper-Evident SHA-256 Audit Trail

Cryptographically signs all evidence dossiers and agent reasoning traces for full regulatory compliance and auditability.

The Five Domains of Autonomous Defense

01

Domain 1 — Simulation & Ingestion

Real-time payment canvas intercepting raw UPI/banking payloads, normalizing metadata, and initializing session state.

02

Domain 2 — ML Hot-Path Inference Engine

Ultra-low latency LightGBM GBDT scoring (<15ms) running on 114 vectorized behavioral, temporal, and velocity features.

03

Domain 3 — LangGraph Multi-Agent Cluster

Intent, Transaction, and Recipient agents perform deep contextual reasoning in parallel with conformal uncertainty bounds.

04

Domain 4 — Deterministic Policy Gatekeeper

Translates probabilistic agent outputs into strict rule directives: ALLOW, ADVISE, CHALLENGE, PAUSE, or BLOCK.

05

Domain 5 — Cognitive Interventions & TruGen Video

Deploys interactive friction, TruGen AI avatar video calls, and voice guidance before payment settlement.

06

Domain 6 — Immutable Compliance & SAR Engine

Tamper-evident cryptographic ledger logging all evidence dossiers with automated Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) generation.

Privacy
