# Advanced Directions Traceability Matrix

## 1. Executive Summary & Chain of Innovation

In advanced technology research, innovation must maintain an unbroken chain of custody. Proposing advanced machine learning architectures, cryptographic protocols, or agentic systems without tracing them back to validated residual failure modes results in ungrounded complexity.

In strict compliance with Part 20 of the Phase 7 mandate, this document establishes the **Five-Node Advanced Traceability Chain**:

$$\text{Residual Problem (RP)} \longrightarrow \text{MVP Limitation (L)} \longrightarrow \text{Advanced Capability (ACAP)} \longrightarrow \text{Priority Tier} \longrightarrow \text{Evolution Horizon}$$

## 3. Verification of Advanced Traceability Integrity

### 3.1 100% Problem-to-Innovation Grounding
Every advanced capability, research frontier, and independent discovery authored in Phase 7 traces directly to an empirical limitation identified in `mvp-limitations.md` or a residual threat modeled in `residual-problems.md`. There are **zero speculative or orphan innovations** in this matrix.

### 3.2 Clear Separation of Horizons
- **Tier 1 (Wave 1)** capabilities rely on existing infrastructure (Kafka, data lakes, push notifications) and carry zero in-line switch clearance risk.
- **Tier 2 (Wave 2)** capabilities leverage localized edge SLMs and internal SOC agents with human-in-the-loop safety boundaries.
- **Tier 3 (Wave 3)** capabilities explore long-term cryptographic consortiums and federated learning, strictly dependent upon multilateral regulatory compacts.
- **Tier 4 (Rejected)** capabilities are formally excluded to preserve system integrity.
