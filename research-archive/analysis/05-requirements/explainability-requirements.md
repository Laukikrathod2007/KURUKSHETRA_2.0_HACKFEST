# Explainability and Transparency Requirements in Scam Defense

## 1. Executive Summary & Epistemic Protocol

In financial technology, **"explainability"** is often treated as a vague, generic aspiration. Technical literature frequently assumes that outputting a SHAP feature attribution graph or an unverified LLM narrative satisfies transparency. In reality, explainability in payment defense is governed by **strict, conflicting statutory mandates** and must serve three completely different human audiences with divergent cognitive needs:
1. **The End User (The Victim)**: Needs intuitive, persuasive causal reasons to break their emotional deception trance.
2. **The Fraud Analyst (SOC Investigator)**: Needs structured, rapid evidence synthesis to triage alerts in under 3 minutes.
3. **The Banking Regulator & Legal Compliance Officer**: Needs auditable, deterministic, and non-discriminatory decision records compliant with Model Risk Management (SR 11-7) and Adverse Action statutes (ECOA).

In strict compliance with Part 8 of the Phase 5 mandate, this document defines the multi-tiered **explainability and transparency requirements** across each of these critical domains.

## 2. The Multi-Tiered Explainability Hierarchy

```text
                     THE THREE EXPLAINABILITY TIERS
                     
  [Tier 1: Consumer-Facing Persuasion Layer]
  - Target: The coerced victim under System 1 panic
  - Nature: Plain language, empathetic, causal, de-biasing
  - Legal Constraint: Must NOT violate AML "tipping-off" criminal laws
  
  [Tier 2: Operational SOC Analyst Layer]
  - Target: Fraud operations investigators under queue pressure
  - Nature: Structured typology indicators, behavioral deltas, graph links
  - Operational Goal: Reduce manual investigation to <3 minutes per alert
  
  [Tier 3: Regulatory & Governance Audit Layer]
  - Target: Bank Model Risk Committees, Federal Reserve, CFPB, Courts
  - Nature: Deterministic feature vectors, parameter sensitivity, Adverse Action codes
  - Legal Mandate: Full compliance with SR 11-7, OCC 2011-12, and ECOA Circular 2022-03
```

## 3. Detailed Explainability Requirement Specifications

### 3.1 REQ-EXP-001: Consumer-Facing Causal Transparency (Victim Persuasion)
- **Statement**: The system MUST be capable of generating plain-language, non-technical explanations of detected risk for the payment user, explicitly highlighting the factual discrepancy or danger pattern (e.g., *"This account was opened 2 days ago and has no prior transaction history with you,"* or *"You are on an active phone call while executing a high-value transfer to an unverified individual"*).
- **Rationale**: Vague disclaimers (*"Security risk detected"*) are dismissed as bureaucracy. Victims require specific, verifiable factual anomalies that challenge the scammer's narrative and prompt System 2 critical evaluation.
- **Traceability Link**: VG-01 (Intent Decoupling), VG-04 (Pre-Coaching Failure); Phase 2 Cognitive Biases.
- **Priority**: **MUST**.
- **Measurable Acceptance Condition**: Consumer-facing explanations use simple, grade-6 reading level vocabulary, contain zero mathematical probabilities or technical model jargon, and articulate at least one observable factual anomaly in under 30 words.
- **Dependencies**: Natural language explanation mapping layer; decision reason codes.
- **Epistemic Uncertainty**: Comprehension and persuasion efficacy across non-native language speakers and elderly cohorts.

---

### 3.2 REQ-EXP-002: AML "Tipping Off" Legal Safe-Harbor Compliance
- **Statement**: All consumer-facing explanations generated by the system MUST be legally formulated to avoid disclosing that a specific counterparty account or UPI ID is under formal law enforcement investigation or subject to a filed Suspicious Activity Report (SAR), relying instead on verifiable transactional, behavioral, and profile characteristics.
- **Rationale**: Under Anti-Money Laundering legislation (UK Proceeds of Crime Act Section 333A, US Bank Secrecy Act 31 U.S.C. 5318, Indian PMLA), "tipping off" a customer regarding an AML investigation is a severe criminal offense punishable by imprisonment. Explanations must persuade without breaching statutory secrecy.
- **Traceability Link**: VG-10 (AML Tipping Off Legal Paradox); REQ-STK-006.
- **Priority**: **MUST**.
- **Measurable Acceptance Condition**: 100% of consumer-facing explanation templates and generated dialog strings pass legal compliance audits, containing zero references to "law enforcement investigations," "SAR filings," or "police watchlists."
- **Dependencies**: Legal compliance review of explanation dictionaries; automated string validation filters.
- **Epistemic Uncertainty**: Evolving regulatory interpretations of what constitutes indirect tipping-off across international jurisdictions.

---

### 3.3 REQ-EXP-003: Operational SOC Analyst Case Synthesis
- **Statement**: For any transaction routed to secondary review, the system MUST generate a structured investigative summary detailing: (a) primary scam typology classification, (b) key behavioral biometric anomalies, (c) counterparty graph risk indicators, (d) deviation from historical baseline, and (e) recommended containment action.
- **Rationale**: Fraud analysts face extreme queue saturation. Presenting raw feature tables or uncurated logs takes 15–30 minutes per case. Structured causal summaries allow analysts to triage cases in under 3 minutes.
- **Traceability Link**: VG-06 (Mule Velocity vs. SOC Triage), Dimension H (Human-in-the-Loop); REQ-STK-005.
- **Priority**: **MUST**.
- **Measurable Acceptance Condition**: The system generates an integrated case dossier for flagged transactions that reduces median analyst investigation and triage time by $\ge 60\%$ in controlled operational time-motion studies.
- **Dependencies**: Automated evidence collation engine; case management integration APIs.
- **Epistemic Uncertainty**: Ensuring generative summarization layers do not produce factual hallucinations on transaction figures.

---

### 3.4 REQ-EXP-004: Regulatory Model Risk Management Auditability (SR 11-7 / OCC 2011-12)
- **Statement**: All algorithmic risk scoring and decision engines MUST produce deterministic, mathematically auditable decision records, including full input feature vectors, model version identifiers, calibrated probability distributions, and local feature attributions, enabling full retrospective reproduction of any decision.
- **Rationale**: Supervisory guidance (Federal Reserve SR 11-7, OCC 2011-12, European Banking Authority) prohibits black-box, non-reproducible models from making automated credit or debit risk decisions in regulated financial institutions.
- **Traceability Link**: VG-08 (Model Governance & Opacity Barrier); Basel Committee Guidelines.
- **Priority**: **MUST**.
- **Measurable Acceptance Condition**: For any audited historical transaction, independent model validators can re-run the recorded input vector through the identical model version archive and reproduce the exact mathematical risk score and decision directive with 100% consistency.
- **Dependencies**: Versioned model registry; deterministic inference runtimes; immutable input vector logging.
- **Epistemic Uncertainty**: Storage costs associated with logging complete high-dimensional feature vectors under high TPS.

---

### 3.5 REQ-EXP-005: Adverse Action Notice Compliance (ECOA / CFPB Circular 2022-03)
- **Statement**: Whenever the system executes an automated transaction block, account restriction, or mandatory payment cancellation, it MUST output at least one and not more than four standardized, legally compliant principal reason codes explaining the specific factual basis of the adverse action to the consumer.
- **Rationale**: Consumer protection statutes (Equal Credit Opportunity Act, CFPB Circular 2022-03, GDPR Article 22) legally prohibit institutions from denying financial services based on opaque algorithmic outputs. Institutions must state the exact, non-discriminatory principal reasons for the denial.
- **Traceability Link**: VG-08 (Model Governance & ECOA Barrier); REQ-STK-006.
- **Priority**: **MUST**.
- **Measurable Acceptance Condition**: Automated transaction declines generate standardized Adverse Action notices containing valid, approved reason codes (e.g., `UNVERIFIED_COUNTERPARTY_AGE`, `EXCEEDS_TEMPORAL_VELOCITY_CEILING`) for 100% of declined transactions.
- **Dependencies**: Mapping layer linking risk factor attributions to regulatory Adverse Action reason code dictionaries.
- **Epistemic Uncertainty**: Alignment of Adverse Action dictionaries across non-credit push payment rails in emerging markets.
