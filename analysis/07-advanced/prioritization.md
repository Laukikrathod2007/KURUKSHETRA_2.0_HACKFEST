# Advanced Directions Prioritization Framework

## 1. Executive Summary & Epistemic Prioritization Criteria

Following the Value vs. Complexity analysis in `value-complexity-analysis.md`, the explored advanced technological directions must be formally organized into **actionable development tiers**.

Prioritization is governed strictly by three criteria:
1. **Evidence Strength**: Does empirical data or peer-reviewed literature confirm the capability's efficacy?
2. **Operational Feasibility**: Can the capability operate within statutory privacy, national switch latency, and banking regulatory constraints?
3. **Marginal Protection Lift**: Does it meaningfully reduce authorized push payment losses beyond the bare-minimum MVP?

## 3. Detailed Justification by Priority Tier

### 3.1 Tier 1: Strongly Justified (Immediate Next Horizon - Wave 1)
- **ACAP-02: Collaborative Co-Guardian Custody**: Non-negotiable defense for elderly and vulnerable retail customers against digital arrest and romance fraud. Low technical risk; immediate life-savings protection lift.
- **ACAP-04: Unsupervised Emerging Anomaly Clustering**: Necessary to eliminate the 30-day supervised retraining blindness to novel zero-day lures. Zero in-line clearance risk; executes as an offline data lake job.
- **ACAP-05: Cross-Rail Smurfing Correlator**: Necessary to eliminate rail arbitrage. Prevents syndicates from evading mobile app friction by splitting funds across IMPS/RTGS channels.
- **RES-03: Cognitive De-Biasing Ergonomics**: Implementing calming blue/green palettes, unhurried 5-second dwell gates, and anti-coaching scripts directly within the MVP's templated UI engine.

---

### 3.2 Tier 2: Promising Strategic Investments (Enterprise Expansion - Wave 2)
- **ACAP-01: Dynamic Socratic De-Biasing Agent**: Deploys an on-device or edge-hosted Small Language Model (SLM) executing 2-turn Socratic inquiry. Sub-second response, zero hallucination via JSON schema decoding.
- **ACAP-07: Agentic SOC Copilot & SAR Automation**: Deploys a ReAct multi-tool agent within the internal fraud operations console, collapsing case triage times from 15 minutes to $\le 2$ minutes and pre-populating regulatory filings.
- **TAI-01: Asynchronous Streaming Graph Neural Networks**: GNN workers continuously ingest national clearing streams, updating 32-dimensional node embeddings stored in fast in-memory caches for instant in-line lookup.
- **GSMA Open Gateway Telco Federation**: Direct carrier integration via Camara Project APIs for network-level call status and SIM swap validation.

---

### 3.3 Tier 3: Experimental Research Frontiers (Consortium Horizon - Wave 3)
- **ACAP-03: Inter-Bank Cryptographic PSI Consortium**: Solves single-bank information myopia via Private Set Intersection. High mathematical readiness, but dependent on multi-institution governance and regulatory charters.
- **ACAP-06: Zero-Knowledge Edge Audio Classifier**: Experimental acoustic stress and synthetic voice detection. Kept in lab research until real-world false-alarm rates are benchmarked across diverse consumer acoustic environments.
- **RES-01: Federated Graph Neural Networks with DP**: Cross-bank collaborative model training without data pooling. High academic value; requires consortium engineering alignment.

---

### 3.4 Tier 4: Formally Rejected Directions (Categorically Prohibited)
- **Real-Time Generative Cloud LLMs in Clearance**: Rejected due to non-deterministic latency (1-3s), hallucination risks, and prompt injection vulnerabilities.
- **Autonomous Debanking & Asset Confiscation (Level 5)**: Rejected due to violation of GDPR Article 22, fair banking statutes, and fundamental civil due process.
- **Continuous Raw Audio Surveillance**: Rejected due to wiretap criminal statutes, privacy violations, and consumer trust destruction.
- **Multi-Hop Blockchain Tracing in In-Line Path**: Rejected as a post-facto investigation tool that adds severe latency without real-time fiat payment interception value.
