# Product Evolution Roadmap: From Bare-Minimum MVP to National Consortium

## 1. Executive Summary & Phased Architecture

A resilient cyber-physical defense system cannot be built in a single monolithic deployment. Attempting to roll out multi-bank cryptographic consortiums and agentic copilots simultaneously on Day 1 guarantees operational collapse.

In strict compliance with Part 17 of the Phase 7 mandate, this document defines the **Phased Evolution Roadmap** for the *Agentic Guardian*. The roadmap organizes capabilities across four progressive deployment horizons:

```text
               THE FOUR-PHASE PRODUCT EVOLUTION ROADMAP
               
  [HORIZON 0: BARE-MINIMUM MVP] ────────► Sub-45ms In-Line GBDT, Pre-Flight Telemetry,
  (Baseline Core Foundation)              Pre-PIN Templated Modal, Sub-60s Mule Alert
            │
            ▼
  [HORIZON 1: HARDENED PERIMETER] ──────► Collaborative Co-Guardians (ACAP-02),
  (Wave 1: Months 3 - 6)                  Cross-Rail Smurfing (ACAP-05), Unsupervised Cluster
            │
            ▼
  [HORIZON 2: INTELLIGENT SCALING] ─────► Edge-Quantized Socratic SLM (ACAP-01),
  (Wave 2: Months 6 - 12)                 Agentic SOC Copilot (ACAP-07), Streaming GNNs
            │
            ▼
  [HORIZON 3: CONSORTIUM DEFENSE] ──────► Cryptographic Inter-Bank PSI (ACAP-03),
  (Wave 3: Months 12 - 24)                Federated Graph Learning, Multi-Hop Cascading Freezes
```

## 2. Detailed Roadmap Horizons

### 2.1 Horizon 0: The Bare-Minimum MVP (Target: Month 0 - 3)
- **Primary Strategic Objective**: Establish the foundational pre-authorization defense perimeter and satisfy national payment switch SLAs.
- **Key Deliverables**:
  - `FEAT-01` Mobile Telemetry Hook SDK (ephemeral touch dynamics, typing cadence).
  - `FEAT-02` Telephony & Screen Sharing Binary Sensor Hooks.
  - `FEAT-03` Sub-45ms Real-Time In-Line Risk Inference Gateway (GBDT).
  - `FEAT-04` Multi-Tier Policy Decision Router (Allow, Inform, Intervene, Hold).
  - `FEAT-05` Typology-Specific De-Biasing Dialog Templates.
  - `FEAT-06` Anti-Habituation Cognitive Attention Gate (5-second dwell time).
  - `FEAT-07` Temporal Cooling-Off Hold Manager.
  - `FEAT-08` Emergency Medical / Life-Safety Bypass Flow.
  - `FEAT-09` Fast Fail-Open Circuit Breaker ($\le 5\text{ms}$).
  - `FEAT-10` Out-of-Band ISO 20022 `camt.056` Mule Containment Dispatcher ($\le 60\text{s}$).
  - `FEAT-12` Immutable Append-Only WORM Decision Logger.

---

### 2.2 Horizon 1: Hardened Operational Perimeter (Wave 1: Months 3 - 6)
- **Primary Strategic Objective**: Close the immediate post-MVP escape vectors (solitary elder isolation, rail arbitrage, and zero-day lure lag).
- **Key Deliverables**:
  - **Collaborative Co-Guardian Dual-Authorization (`ACAP-02`)**: Opt-in trusted family co-signing for transfers exceeding ₹50,000.
  - **Cross-Rail Smurfing & Multi-Channel Correlator (`ACAP-05`)**: Kafka-based unified transaction velocity tracking across UPI, IMPS, RTGS, and card rails.
  - **Unsupervised Emerging Anomaly Clustering (`ACAP-04`)**: Daily HDBSCAN clustering discovering novel zero-day scam signatures within 12 hours.
  - **Cognitive Ergonomics Modernization (`RES-03`)**: Implementation of calming de-biasing color palettes and anti-coaching linguistic patterns.

---

### 2.3 Horizon 2: Intelligent Enterprise Scaling (Wave 2: Months 6 - 12)
- **Primary Strategic Objective**: Scale human investigator capacity and overcome live scammer phone coaching via edge machine learning.
- **Key Deliverables**:
  - **Dynamic Socratic De-Biasing Agent (`ACAP-01`)**: Edge-quantized Small Language Model (Gemma-2-2B / Llama-3.2-1B) executing on-device Socratic counter-framing dialogues.
  - **Agentic SOC Copilot & SAR Engine (`ACAP-07`)**: Autonomous multi-tool evidence retrieval, 2-hop graph visualization, and automated SAR narrative generation.
  - **Asynchronous Streaming Graph Embeddings (`TAI-01`)**: Temporal Graph Networks computing node embeddings across national clearing streams for in-line cache lookup.
  - **Carrier Network Federation (`REQ-STK-007`)**: Commercial GSMA Open Gateway integration for real-time network-level call status and SIM swap attestation.

---

### 2.4 Horizon 3: Consortium & Decentralized Resilience (Wave 3: Months 12 - 24)
- **Primary Strategic Objective**: Break single-bank information silos and establish an automated multi-bank containment network.
- **Key Deliverables**:
  - **Cryptographic Inter-Bank PSI Consortium (`ACAP-03`)**: Zero-knowledge Private Set Intersection querying shared mule indices across participating financial institutions.
  - **Automated Multi-Hop Inter-Bank Chasing (`Recovery Direction 2`)**: Cryptographically signed cascading freeze advisories pursuing stolen funds across multi-bank mule layers.
  - **Statutory Mandatory Reimbursement Engine (`Recovery Direction 3`)**: Automated 50/50 liability partitioning and dispute arbitration compliant with UK PSR / global standards.
  - **Federated Graph Neural Networks with Differential Privacy (`RES-01`)**: Cross-institutional collaborative model training without customer data pooling.
